## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Product and document scope

This bank covers the FV hydraulic double-acting self-centering clamp series: FVA
small slide-driven models, FVD high-capacity and extended-stroke slide-driven models,
and FVC long-stroke link-mechanism models. It includes operating principles, model
grammar, specifications, clamp-force relationships, dimensions, jaw-plate design,
installation, hydraulic circuits, cautions, maintenance, and the applicable control
valves and accessories included in the PDF.

Commercial warranty and sales-network material remain in the source inventory but
are excluded from core capability questions. Common hydraulic cautions and notation
references must use `DOCUMENT_COMMON` binding with a page-bounded local scope so
they cannot be confused with FV-local requirements.

### 2.2 Model Grammar

The three FV product families use the following printed field orders. Optional
fields remain blank when no symbol is selected.

#### FVA small slide-driven family

`FVA<ClampForceCode><DesignNo>`

| Field | Legal values | Meaning and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| ClampForceCode | `040`, `063`, `100` | At 7 MPa supply pressure and 15 mm clamp-point height, the total two-jaw clamp forces are 5.2, 8.6, and 14.0 kN respectively. |
| DesignNo | `1` | Product series/design number listed by this PDF. |

#### FVD slide-driven family

`FVD<ClampForceCode><DesignNo>-<StrokeOption?>`

| Field | Legal values | Meaning and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| ClampForceCode | `160`, `250`, `400` | Selects the printed clamp-force/body-size class; the exact force remains conditional on supply pressure and clamp-point height. |
| DesignNo | `0` | Product series/design number listed by this PDF. |
| StrokeOption | blank, `L` | Blank is the standard stroke; `L` is the extended-stroke version. |

The specification table distinguishes the stroke option within each FVD body size.
For `FVD1600`, the standard and `L` versions have single-side strokes of 6 mm and
12 mm respectively, and maximum clamp-point heights at 7 MPa of 60 mm and 100 mm.

#### FVC long-stroke link-mechanism family

`FVC<OutputCode><DesignNo>`

| Field | Legal values | Meaning and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| OutputCode | `063`, `100`, `160` | At 7 MPa, the clamp-actuator outputs are 6.6, 11.1, and 16.4 kN respectively. Clamp-actuator output is not itself the final jaw clamp force. |
| DesignNo | `0` | Product series/design number listed by this PDF. |

Positive grammar cases:

- `FVA0631`
- `FVD1600-L`
- `FVC1000`

Negative grammar cases and reasons:

- `FVA0630`: FVA design number `0` is not listed; the printed design number is `1`.
- `FVD1601-L`: FVD design number `1` is not listed; the printed design number is `0`.
- `FVD1600-X`: stroke option `X` is not listed.
- `FVC2500`: FVC output code `250` is not listed.
- `FVC1000-L`: FVC has no printed stroke-option field.
